# Relevant distribution

Choose a channel because its audience needs the resource, not because of a claimed domain-authority score. Verify current eligibility, submission rules, cost, and whether the listing actually fits the project. A repository alone may not qualify for an app marketplace.

Prepare a truthful description, link, screenshots, and a reason the audience benefits. Record channel, checked rules URL/date, proposed action, submitted status, accepted status, and verified destination separately. Do not promise acceptance rates or impose a submissions-per-day ritual.

For a proposed personal message, reference a relevant task or resource and offer a specific useful contribution. Use only genuine credentials and realistic timelines. Sending emails, submissions, or social posts requires authorization; preparation alone is not consent.

Evaluate resulting visits and useful actions. A backlink or listing is not proof of traffic, indexation, or product adoption. Avoid paid link schemes and mass low-value submissions.

Source: [Google link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).
