# Links that help discovery and readers

Inspect important destination pages and the paths customers use to reach them. Use real anchors with href and descriptive text; verify the target responds and is the intended canonical page.

Add a link when it answers a likely next question or supports the current explanation. Avoid arbitrary per-page quotas, exact-match anchor repetition, or a universal maximum click depth. Navigation, related resources, and contextual links serve different needs.

To investigate orphan pages, compare a scoped crawl/route inventory with internal links and sitemap URLs. A one-page preflight cannot prove a site-wide orphan problem. Mark incomplete crawl coverage explicitly.

Source: [Google crawlable links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).
