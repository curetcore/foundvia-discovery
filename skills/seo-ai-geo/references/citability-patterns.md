# Clear, verifiable answers

These are editorial patterns, not measured citation guarantees. Start with a direct answer to the reader's actual question, then explain conditions and limitations. Use numbered steps when order matters, tables for genuine comparisons, and descriptive headings for navigation.

Support factual claims with checked primary sources or original evidence. Include tested examples, the version/date relevant to the result, and what was not measured. Do not cite invented studies or claim that a particular format forces an assistant to extract your answer.

Review the resource without search traffic: would a real customer still find it useful? Record manual search/citation snapshots separately from referral sessions. Neither one establishes stable rankings.

Source: [Google AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).
