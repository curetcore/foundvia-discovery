# Real locations and service areas

Maintain one verified record per real location: name, public contact details, staffed address when applicable, hours, destination URL, and supported services. Keep service-area coverage distinct from physical offices. Do not publish a private home address when policy requires hiding it.

A location page should help a customer visit or obtain service: access, actual services, contact, hours, and useful local evidence. Do not generate pages by replacing city names or invent local reviews/statistics. Return a real not-found response for nonexistent locations.

Use the relevant LocalBusiness subtype with truthful visible properties; retain consistent organization/location identity where useful. Geo markup does not guarantee local-pack ranking. Self-serving LocalBusiness/Organization reviews do not qualify for Google's review stars, including embedded third-party review widgets. Do not disguise the business as Product to bypass this restriction.

Acceptance: verify details with owner records, open each affected route, validate eligible schema, and compare any authorized Business Profile information. Profile creation/updates and customer replies require authorization.

Sources: [business representation](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177), [LocalBusiness schema](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business), [review eligibility](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet).
